What Khelbro is

Khelbro is a skill-based Ludo platform where players create a battle, agree an amount, play a match, and report the result.

How it works

Create a battle or take an open one. The creator shares an 8-digit room code. Both players join, play, and report the outcome. Winnings are credited once both reports agree.

Skill, not chance

Ludo rewards planning: which token to move, when to take a capture, when to hold a safe square, and how to manage risk across four tokens.

Fairness

Every battle needs both players to agree the result. Conflicting reports are reviewed rather than paid out automatically.
