Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 August 2026.

What we collect

Your mobile number, the display name and avatar you choose, an optional email address, and — if you request a withdrawal — the identity details required for verification.

How we use it

To operate your account, run battles, settle results, process withdrawals, and contact you about your account.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help operate the platform, and where the law requires disclosure.

Retention

Account and transaction records are retained for as long as your account is open and thereafter as required by law.

Your choices

You can update your profile at any time and request deletion of your account by contacting support.

Security

Access is protected by one-time passwords. Never share your OTP with anyone, including anyone claiming to be from Khelbro.

Questions about this page? Contact support.
