Responsible Gaming

Last updated 28 August 2026.

Play for entertainment

Treat entry amounts as the cost of entertainment, never as a way to make money or recover a loss.

Set your own limits

Decide before you start how much time and money you are willing to spend, and stop when you reach it.

Warning signs

Playing longer than intended, chasing losses, borrowing to play, hiding play from family, or feeling anxious when not playing.

Take a break

If any of the above sounds familiar, stop playing and contact support to have your account paused.

Age restriction

Khelbro is strictly for users aged 18 and over.

Getting help

If gaming is affecting your life or finances, seek help from a qualified counsellor or a support helpline in your area.
