Common questions

Is Khelbro free?

Yes. Every mode is free to play and there is no real-money wagering on this site.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play immediately. Signing in only saves your stats and leaderboard position.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes — pass-and-play shares one device between 2 or 4 players. Online multiplayer is planned.

How hard is the computer?

Three levels. Easy moves almost randomly, medium prefers captures and safety, hard scores every legal move before choosing.

My game froze. What now?

Refresh the page. Game state is saved locally, so you will be offered the option to resume.
